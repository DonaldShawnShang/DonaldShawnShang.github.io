🔥About Me

I am a doctoral student (D.Eng.) in Computer Technology at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University, and a joint doctoral student and research assistant at Pengcheng Laboratory. I work on AI security, with a focus on the security of LLM-based agents.

  • Current focus: LLM agent security. Agents that read untrusted content and act through tools can be steered by injected instructions. I study how these attacks work in practice, and how to detect and stop them.
  • Previously: in-vehicle network security. Lightweight cross-layer intrusion detection for Automotive Ethernet, published as AutoGuard (Computer Networks, 2026).

I received my B.Eng. in Information Security from the School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Jiaotong University, in 2024. Outside papers, I enjoy hands-on security: I was a global runner-up at the BCTF × DEF CON 30 Auto-driving Contest.
