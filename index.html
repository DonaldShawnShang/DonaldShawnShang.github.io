01About

I am an Engineering Doctorate (Eng.D) student in Computer Technology at Sun Yat-sen University, and a research assistant in the Department of New Networks at Pengcheng Laboratory, where I am a jointly trained Eng.D student. Before that, I received my B.Eng. in Information Security (Secret Protection) from Beijing Jiaotong University in 2024.

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and system security, along two lines:

  • In-vehicle network security Learning-based intrusion detection for CAN and Automotive Ethernet (TSN, CAN-over-UDP, SOME/IP) that works across protocol layers while staying small enough for in-vehicle controllers.
  • Security of LLM-based agents Red-teaming, injection attacks, and practical guardrails for LLM agents that act through tools.

I also enjoy hands-on security: I was a global runner-up in the BCTF × DEF CON 30 Auto-driving Contest and won third prize in the 16th National Cybersecurity Competition.

Computer Networks 2026

AutoGuard: Unified and Lightweight Cross-Layer Intrusion Detection for Automotive Ethernet via Contextual Traffic Analysis

Wentao Shang, Shuyuan Jin, Weihong Han

Computer Networks (Elsevier), Vol. 286, Article 112394, 2026.CCF-B

TL;DRA single byte-level Transformer over hex-bigram tokens jointly monitors TSN streams, CAN-over-UDP and SOME/IP traffic, covering eleven attack types without protocol-specific feature engineering. Teacher–student distillation shrinks it from 132M to 1.5M parameters (~90×) for deployment on zonal controllers.
